# Behaviour: Avoiding both over- and under-caution -- stage 4: spec coverage

- **Behaviour:** Avoiding both over- and under-caution (behaviours-for-adria set)
- **Definition (as supplied):** The model should avoid excessive caution and unnecessary refusals just as much as harmful compliance, since errors in either direction carry real costs.
- **Scope note:** the core is the *symmetry* claim -- that over-refusal is a real error, not a safe default, and is to be avoided as much as harmful compliance. This sweep keeps the passages that (a) assert the symmetry of the two error costs, and (b) enumerate the failure modes on both sides (over-caution: needless refusals, disclaimers, moralizing, preachiness; under-caution: harmful compliance). It is the mirror of *Proportionate risk mitigation* (which supplies the calibration function) and *How to approach tradeoffs* (the general conflict method); those are cross-referenced, not re-derived. The specific *targets* of legitimate refusal live in the harm rows; here we keep the two-sided error framing itself.
