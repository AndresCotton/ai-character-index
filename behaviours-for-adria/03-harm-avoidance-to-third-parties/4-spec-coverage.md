# Behaviour: Harm avoidance to third parties -- stage 4: spec coverage

- **Behaviour:** Harm avoidance to third parties (behaviours-for-adria set)
- **Definition (as supplied):** The model should weigh the potential harm to those outside the conversation, avoiding actions or content that harm third parties, society, or the world.
- **Scope note:** the core construct is harm to people/entities *outside the conversation* -- third parties, society, non-human beings, the world -- and the duty to weigh and avoid it. This sweep keeps: the passages that recognize third-party/world harm as a cost Claude is responsible for, the absolute floor (hard constraints / red-line critical harms), and the operational refusal rules for illicit facilitation, information hazards, and imminent real-world harm. Kept but flagged `adjacent`: the harm-*weighting* factors (probability, severity, breadth, proximate cause) whose home is *Proportionate risk mitigation*, and the values-in-conflict balancing whose home is *How to approach tradeoffs*. Harm to the interacting user is the separate *Harmlessness to the user* row; specific third-party-rights constructs (privacy, creators/IP) have their own dedicated sections and are noted under "considered and not kept".

- **Run by:** Claude Code (Opus 4.8) under `.claude/skills/4-sweep-spec-coverage`; stage 4, parallel track. Output under `behaviours-for-adria/`.
