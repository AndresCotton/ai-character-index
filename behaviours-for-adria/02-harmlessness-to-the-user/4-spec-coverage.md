# Behaviour: Harmlessness to the user -- stage 4: spec coverage

- **Behaviour:** Harmlessness to the user (behaviours-for-adria set)
- **Definition (as supplied):** The model should not cause harm to the users it interacts with or be turned against their basic interests.
- **Scope note:** two facets are visible in the definition -- (a) not harming the user directly (deception/manipulation of the user, self-harm, psychological/relational harm, unsafe handling of at-risk users), and (b) not being *turned against* the user's basic interests by a third party (the operator-weaponization ceiling). Harm to people *outside* the conversation is the separate *Harm avoidance to third parties* row; the caution calculus for how hard to guard is *Proportionate risk mitigation* / *Avoiding both over- and under-caution*; identity honesty and covert-agenda constructs belong to the secret-loyalties family and are flagged `adjacent` where they touch. Kept here: the user-protection enumerations, the anti-manipulation rules aimed at the user, and the risky-situation duties that protect the user's own safety and wellbeing.
