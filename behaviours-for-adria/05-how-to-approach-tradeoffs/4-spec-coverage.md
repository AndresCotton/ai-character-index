# Behaviour: How to approach tradeoffs -- stage 4: spec coverage

- **Behaviour:** How to approach tradeoffs (behaviours-for-adria set)
- **Definition (as supplied):** When values or goals conflict, the model should navigate the tradeoff through structured prioritization rather than abandoning one consideration entirely.
- **Scope note:** the core is the *method* for resolving conflicts between values/goals -- an ordered-but-holistic prioritization that weighs considerations against each other instead of zeroing one out. This is distinct from the individual values being traded off (their own rows) and from the specific harm/benefit *calibration* function (Proportionate risk mitigation) and the specific over-/under-refusal symptom (Avoiding both over- and under-caution); those are kept only where they carry the tradeoff *machinery* and are flagged. Kept here: the priority ordering and the "holistic not strict" rule, the balancing heuristics (thoughtful senior employee, dual newspaper, holistic judgment), the model spec's chain-of-command-as-conflict-resolver framing, and the explicit outcome orderings that demonstrate structured prioritization.
